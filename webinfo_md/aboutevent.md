Background and Rationale
As part of the Platinum Jubilee Celebrations of the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (established in 1951), the Department of Civil Engineering is organizing the International Conference on Infrastructure Sustainability and Resilience Forum : INFRASURE 2027 from 22 to 24 January 2027, at  IIT Kharagpur extension centre Rajarhat,  IIT Kharagpur campus, Kolkata
Climate change, rapid urbanization, population growth, and increasing pressure on natural resources are creating unprecedented challenges for civil infrastructure and water systems worldwide. Depletion of natural resources, green gas emissions,  increase in pollution, rising frequencies of floods, droughts, riverbank erosion, water scarcity, and extreme weather events highlight the urgent need for sustainable and climate-resilient engineering solutions. Sustainable civil and water engineering aims to develop infrastructure that not only meets present societal needs but also enhances environmental protection, resource efficiency, long-term resilience and net zero.
Natural resources including water security have emerged as a critical global concern, requiring innovative approaches for sustainable infrastructure planning, water management, and risk mitigation. Climate-resilient development emphasizes the integration of adaptive water infrastructure, advanced technologies, and sustainable management practices to ensure reliable water natural resources availability under changing climatic conditions.
Nature-based solutions, including preservation of natural resources, construction materials, ecosystem restoration, river rehabilitation, green infrastructure, and watershed management, are increasingly recognized as effective strategies for enhancing resilience while delivering environmental and social co-benefits.
This conference is conceived as a high-energy, idea-driven forum where science meets practice, technology meets policy, and innovation meets responsibility. More than a conventional academic conference, this conference aims to be a confluence of ideas, solutions, and partnerships, bringing together thinkers and doers committed to shaping the future with sustainable and climate-resilient Civil and Water Resources Engineering at local, regional and global scales.
Objectives
The primary objectives of INFRASURE 2027 are:
1.    To provide a forum for presenting cutting-edge research and innovations in sustainable civil and water engineering.
2.    To promote the development of climate-resilient infrastructure capable of withstanding extreme weather events, floods, droughts, and other climate-related risks.
3.    To explore innovative technologies, digital tools, artificial intelligence, and smart monitoring systems for sustainable infrastructure and water resource management.
4.    To promote multidisciplinary research for nature-based solutions and environmentally sustainable approaches for preservation of construction materials, net zero, flood management, river restoration, groundwater recharge, and ecosystem conservation.
5.    To encourage early-career researchers and students through dedicated sessions, mentoring, and awards.
6.    To strengthen collaboration among academia, government agencies, industries, and international organizations for addressing global water and civil engineering infrastructure challenges.
 
Conference Focus Areas and Core Themes
INFRASURE (Infrastructure Sustainability and Resilience Forum) is a premier platform dedicated to advancing sustainable, resilient, and climate-adaptive infrastructure systems. The forum brings together researchers, engineers, policymakers, industry professionals, students and practitioners to exchange knowledge, showcase innovations, and discuss emerging challenges in civil infrastructure including roads, geotechnical, water resources, environmental sustainability, and disaster resilience. Emphasizing climate-resilient development, green infrastructure, water security, digital transformation, and nature-based solutions, INFRASURE aims to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and promote engineering solutions that support sustainable growth, environmental stewardship, and resilient communities for future generations.
