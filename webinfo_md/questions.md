# INFRASURE 2027 — Participant Perspective Questions

Every question a student, researcher, or attendee would ask before deciding to register.
Organized by the **page** that should answer them. Use this as a checklist when
building each page.

---

## 1. Home / Landing Page

The landing page is the first impression. It should answer at a glance:

- [x] What is INFRASURE 2027?
- [x] Who is organizing it?
- [x] When and where is it?
- [x] Who should attend?
- [x] What are the 9 themes?
- [x] How do I register?
- [x] Is registration open yet?
- [x] What is the Platinum Jubilee connection?

## 5. Call for Contributions / Submission

### Eligibility
- [ ] Who can submit a paper or poster?
- [ ] Can undergraduate students submit?
- [ ] Can industry professionals submit?
- [ ] Can someone submit who is NOT attending in person?
- [ ] Do all co-authors need to be registered?

### Format
- [ ] What are the submission formats — PPT, Poster, or full paper?
- [ ] What is the difference between a PPT submission and a Poster submission?
- [ ] Is there a template or format guide? Where do I download it?
- [ ] What are the slide dimensions for PPT?
- [ ] What are the poster dimensions (A0, A1, portrait, landscape)?
- [ ] What is the word limit for the abstract?
- [ ] What sections should the abstract contain?
- [ ] What referencing style should I use?
- [ ] Can I include figures, tables, or equations?
- [ ] What file formats are accepted (PDF, PPTX, PNG)?
- [ ] Is there a file size limit?

### Limits & Rules
- [ ] How many submissions can one person make?
- [ ] Can I submit to two different themes?
- [ ] Can I submit the same work as both PPT and Poster?
- [ ] Can I have different co-authors on different submissions?
- [ ] Must the presenting author be the first author?
- [ ] What if I want to withdraw my submission?

### Review Process
- [ ] How does the two-round review process work?
- [ ] Who reviews my submission — a single person or a panel?
- [ ] Is the review double-blind?
- [ ] What happens in Round 1?
- [ ] What kind of feedback will I receive?
- [ ] What happens in Round 2 after I address the feedback?
- [ ] How long does each round take?
- [ ] Can I submit a revised version before the deadline?
- [ ] What does "Selected" mean — can I present AND publish?
- [ ] What does "Not Selected" mean — can I still attend?
- [ ] Is there an appeal process if my submission is rejected?

### Plagiarism & Ethics
- [ ] Is there a plagiarism check? What tool is used?
- [ ] What is the acceptable similarity percentage?
- [ ] Is AI-assisted writing allowed? Do I need to disclose it?
- [ ] Must the work be original and not submitted elsewhere?
- [ ] What happens if plagiarism is detected after acceptance?

## 7. Fees & Payment

### Fee Structure
- [ ] What is the registration fee for Students (Indian ₹)?
- [ ] What is the fee for Faculty / Academic (Indian ₹)?
- [ ] What is the fee for Industry professionals (Indian ₹)?
- [ ] What is the fee for foreign participants (USD)?
- [ ] Is there a separate fee for in-person vs online?
- [ ] Does the fee cover both participation AND publication?
- [ ] Is there a discount for presenting vs non-presenting attendees?
- [ ] If I have 2 submissions, do I pay the fee twice?
- [ ] What does the fee include (meals, tea, kit, proceedings, certificate)?
- [ ] Is GST included or additional?

### Early Bird
- [ ] What is the early-bird fee vs regular fee?
- [ ] When does the early-bird period end?
- [ ] How do I qualify for the early-bird rate?

### Payment
- [ ] What payment methods are accepted (UPI, net banking, card, demand draft)?
- [ ] Is there an online payment gateway? Which one?
- [ ] Can I pay via institutional funding / purchase order?
- [ ] Will I receive a receipt / invoice?
- [ ] What happens if my payment fails or is debited twice?
- [ ] Can I get a GST invoice for my institution?

### Refund & Cancellation
- [ ] What is the refund policy?
- [ ] Can I get a full refund if I cancel before a certain date?
- [ ] Is there a partial refund window?
- [ ] Can I transfer my registration to a colleague?
- [ ] What if the conference is cancelled or moved online due to force majeure?
- [ ] Who do I contact for refund issues?
