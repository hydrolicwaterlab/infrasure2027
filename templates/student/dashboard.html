How the two rounds work

  • Submit Round 1 — just a title and abstract under any of the nine themes.
  • The theme incharge routes it to a reviewer, or reviews it themselves.
  • Round 1 is decided: Selected for Round 2, or Not Selected.
  • If selected, you choose your format — PPT or Poster — for each submission.
  • Round 2: Poster teams save a revised title and abstract; PPT teams upload a PDF.
  • A final decision — Selected or Not Selected — ends the process for that submission.
