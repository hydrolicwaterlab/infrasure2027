Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data {{ config.short_title }} collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it. It applies to all visitors and registered participants.

1. Data we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, institute/university, department, and designation required to create a participant account.
  • Registration details: category, mode of participation, registration fees paid, and any information you provide in the participant details form.
  • Submission information: abstracts, PPTs, posters, and any co-author details submitted through the portal.
  • Technical data: a small session cookie (session_token, valid 6 hours) used to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely for the legitimate purposes of this conference: processing registration and payments, managing submissions and the peer-review process, sharing conference updates, issuing certificates and proceedings, and meeting statutory reporting requirements. Your data is not sold or used for commercial marketing.

3. Who can access your data

Access is strictly need-to-know: the organising committee and administrative staff by necessity; theme incharges and reviewers only to the extent required for reviewing your submission; and the publisher (e.g. Springer) only if your paper is selected for publication. Legal and regulatory authorities may access data where required by Indian law.

4. Data retention

Your data is retained for the duration of the conference and for as long as required by applicable records and tax regulations. After that, it is securely deleted or anonymised unless you consent otherwise.

5. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and equivalently GDPR for EU participants), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards — including encrypted transmission, restricted access, and secure storage — to protect your data from unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

7. Contact

For any privacy question, correction, or deletion request, write to {{ config.email }}. We will respond within 30 days.

Code of Conduct

{{ config.short_title }} is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for all participants, regardless of background, identity, nationality, or academic standing.

1. Expected behaviour

  • Treat every participant, organiser, speaker, and staff member with respect and courtesy.
  • Engage constructively in scientific and professional discussions.
  • Follow all instructions of session chairs and the organising committee.
  • Uphold academic integrity — including honest authorship and honest reporting of results.

2. Unacceptable behaviour

  • Harassment in any form — verbal, physical, or written — including harassment based on gender, race, religion, caste, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • Discrimination, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted attention of any kind.
  • Disruption of sessions or conference activities.
  • Plagiarism, data fabrication, or other academic misconduct.

3. Reporting an incident

If you experience or witness a violation, please report it to any member of the organising committee, or directly at {{ config.email }}. Reports will be handled confidentially and proportionately.

4. Internal Committee (POSH Act)

As an event hosted by IIT Kharagpur, this conference operates under the institution’s Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act, 2013. Complaints of sexual harassment may be referred to this committee for appropriate action.

5. Consequences

Depending on the severity of the violation, consequences may include a warning, exclusion from sessions, or removal from the conference. In cases of serious misconduct, removal applies without refund of any fees paid.

Publication Ethics

These standards apply to every abstract, PPT, poster, and paper submitted to or published under {{ config.short_title }}.

1. Originality

All submitted work must be original, previously unpublished, and must not be under consideration elsewhere at the time of submission. Text similarity must be within the acceptable threshold (currently <10%) when checked with standard plagiarism-detection software (e.g. Turnitin / iThenticate).

2. Honest reporting

Authors must accurately represent their data, methods, and results. Fabrication or falsification of data is a serious offence.

3. Authorship

All listed authors must have made a meaningful scholarly contribution and must have consented to be authors. The presenting author must be authorised by all co-authors.

4. AI-assisted writing

Use of AI tools in writing shall be disclosed where required. Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, integrity, and originality of the work. AI tools may not be listed as authors.

5. Review integrity

Reviewers evaluate submissions objectively, on scholarly merit, and treat all submissions as confidential. Conflicts of interest are disclosed and managed.

6. Plagiarism after acceptance

If plagiarism or misconduct is detected at any stage — including after acceptance or publication — the submission may be withdrawn or retracted, and the matter may be reported to the author’s institution.

7. Copyright

Authors retain copyright of their work and grant the organisers permission to publish accepted work in the ISBN proceedings and any associated Springer publication under the agreed terms.

Submission Guidelines

1. Who can submit

Students (UG, PG, PhD, and postdoctoral researchers), academics, scientists, and industry professionals. At least one author must register for the conference for a submission to be considered.

2. Formats

  • PPT: Presentation slides, submitted as a PDF or PPTX using the provided template.
  • Poster: A0 / A1 portrait, submitted as a PDF or PNG using the provided template (dimensions TBD).

3. Abstract

The abstract should clearly state the objective, method, results, and significance of the work. Word and section limits will be specified in the submission portal (limits TBD). Figures and tables may be included in the abstract where permitted.

4. Limits & rules

  • You may submit up to two submissions.
  • Each submission may target only one theme.
  • You may not submit the same work in both PPT and Poster formats.
  • Submissions must follow the referencing style specified on the portal.
  • Accepted file formats: PDF, PPTX, PNG (file size limit TBD).

5. Review process

Submissions undergo a two-round review. In Round 1, reviewers evaluate the abstract and provide feedback. In Round 2, you submit a revised version after addressing the feedback, following which a final selection decision is made. Feedback timelines follow the deadlines on the Important Dates / Call for Contributions sections.

6. Withdrawal

You may withdraw a submission at any time before the final decision by writing to {{ config.email }}.
